Roozlet 0.1.1 improves date formatting, bilingual header alignment and month navigation.

- **Settings → Language & dates → Prefer numerical form of dates** uses `year/month/day`
  in tooltips, selected-day details and clipboard copies, respecting the chosen digit style.
- The desktop widget's **Copy numerical date** submenu copies Persian, Gregorian,
  Hijri or all three dates independently of the preference. Unavailable official
  Hijri dates retain their coverage message.
- The Persian card title is right-aligned with the settings gear on the left;
  English uses the title on the left and gear on the right.
- Right-click **Today**, or focus it and press Shift+F10, to choose from all 12
  Persian months and navigate years. Normal left-click still returns to today.

## Install

```sh
rooz --quit
sudo apt install ./roozlet_0.1.1-1_all.deb
rooz
```

Existing settings are preserved; numerical dates are initially disabled.
